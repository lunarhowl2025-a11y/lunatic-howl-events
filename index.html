ABOUT US

Lunar Howl is a non-profit association (feitelijke vereniging) dedicated to nurturing emerging talent in the electronic music scene. Founded in Borgloon, Belgium.

We believe in the raw power of underground culture. Through coaching, showcases, workshops, and events, we create a safe environment where talent can grow.

Our mission: support the next generation of techno artists.
